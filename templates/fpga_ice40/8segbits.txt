Alchitry IO 7-Segment Display Bit Mapping (Active Low)
------------------------------------------------------
0 = dot (decimal point)
1 = MM  (middle horizontal)
2 = LT  (top-left vertical)
3 = LB  (bottom-left vertical)
4 = MB  (bottom horizontal)
5 = RB  (bottom-right vertical)
6 = RT  (top-right vertical)
7 = MT  (top horizontal)
